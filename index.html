The name

Descartes said cogito. We say cogitamus.

"I think" is where Descartes began. We start one step further along, with "we think," because the hardest questions of this decade are not solved by anyone alone.

Cogitamus makes videos about the forces changing the world fastest: the machines we are building, the ideas we inherit, and the power that decides how both are used. Each piece starts with a question and follows the evidence, even when it leads somewhere uncomfortable.
